About

I work at the intersection of curriculum design, educational technology, and inclusive education. My academic training at IER, University of Dhaka, is grounded in frameworks like TPACK, SAMR, ADDIE, and Universal Design for Learning — which I apply directly in the field, from BRAC classrooms to research on teacher technological readiness.

Alongside research and teaching, I produce accessible learning content: Bangla audiobooks, voice-over narration, and English-learning scripts reaching underserved learners across Bangladesh.
